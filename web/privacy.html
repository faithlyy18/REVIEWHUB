ReviewHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

ReviewHub ("the app") is an educational review and module-management application built for the BS Criminology curriculum. This page explains what information the app accesses and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign in, we collect your name, email address, and role (student, instructor, or admin) to identify your account within the app.
  • Google account information (Sign-In): If you connect your Google account to upload modules, we access your basic profile information (name, email) to confirm your identity for that session.
  • Google Drive access: When an instructor or admin connects Google Drive to upload a module, the app requests permission to view and select files from that Google account's Drive, solely to let you pick a PDF or DOCX file to attach to a module. The app does not access, read, or store any other files in your Drive.
  • App data: Quiz results, module content, and course-related activity are stored in our Firebase (Firestore) database to support the app's core features.

How We Use Information

  • To authenticate users and enforce role-based access (student, instructor, admin)
  • To let instructors and admins attach Google Drive files to course modules
  • To track quiz results, retake requests, and academic progress within the app
  • To operate and improve the app's core features

Information Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Data accessed through Google Sign-In or Google Drive is used only within ReviewHub for the purposes described above.

Data Storage

App data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore). We do not store your Google account password, and we do not retain a copy of files you select from Google Drive beyond the shareable link needed to display them in a module.

Your Choices

You can disconnect your Google account's access to ReviewHub at any time through your Google Account's security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
